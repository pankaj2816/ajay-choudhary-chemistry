Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about batches, offline centers, test series, and study materials.

How can I enroll in Ajay Sir’s regular batches at the coaching centers?

You can submit the admission inquiry form on this page or visit any of the three coaching centers directly during counseling hours (4:00 PM – 7:30 PM). Our academic counselors will guide you through batch timings and seat availability.

Are separate doubt clearing sessions available for students?

Yes! In addition to classroom lectures, dedicated 45-minute doubt resolution clinics are conducted after every lecture across Catalyst, Apex, and Prerana centers.

Can students access question papers and verified solutions for free?

Absolutely. All enrolled and registered students can browse, preview, and download chapter test papers, previous years’ questions, and detailed verified solutions directly from this website.

How are Practical Chemistry & Salt Analysis classes organized?

Practical chemistry workshops are held on weekends in fully equipped partner laboratory facilities, covering systematic cation/anion analysis, volumetric titration, and board viva preparation.
