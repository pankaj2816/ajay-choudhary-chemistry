Organic Chemistry

Markovnikov Addition to Alkenes

High-Yield Topic
Chemical Equation / Profile:
CH₃–CH=CH₂ + HBr ⟶ CH₃–CH(Br)–CH₃ (Major Product)

Step-by-Step Conceptual Mechanism:

1Step 1: Double bond π-electrons attack H⁺ electrophile.
2Step 2: Generation of more stable 2° carbocation (hyperconjugation + inductive stabilization).
3Step 3: Rapid nucleophilic attack by Br⁻ giving 2-Bromopropane.
Key Conceptual Rule:

The hydrogen atom bonds to the carbon with the greatest number of hydrogen atoms.
