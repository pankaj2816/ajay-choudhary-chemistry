02About

Concepts first. The marks follow.

Chemistry becomes effortless when concepts are understood, not just memorized.

Ajay Choudhary Sir began his teaching career with a single goal: to dismantle the widespread myth that Chemistry requires endless mechanical memorization. Over the past 8+ years, he has guided more than 5,000 students across Class 11, Class 12, CBSE/ISC Boards, JEE Main, and NEET.

Ajay Sir currently teaches Class 11 and 12 Chemistry at three schools in Alwar district, Rajasthan. He is known for clear mental maps of organic reaction mechanisms, explaining the reasons behind inorganic trends, making physical chemistry numericals simple, and hands-on practical and salt analysis sessions.

04Method

How a chapter is taught

A 6-step systematic methodology that turns complex chemical reactions into intuitive, logical understanding.

  1. 01

    First-Principles Foundation

    Deconstruct atomic structures, hybridization, and electronic configurations before tackling complex problems.

  2. 02

    Visual Reaction Mechanisms

    Master electron movements with curved-arrow notation instead of memorizing hundreds of disconnected equations.

  3. 03

    Lab Practical Integration

    Correlate textbook equations with real laboratory precipitate colors, flame tests, and titration endpoints.

  4. 04

    Graduated Multi-Tier Practice

    Progressive problem solving from NCERT & Board exemplars to high-yield JEE Main and NEET DPPs.

  5. 05

    Mistake Analysis & Doubts

    Regular unit test reviews, identifying conceptual traps, calculation errors, and examiner tricks.

  6. 06

    Board Exam Presentation Tactics

    Structured methods for 3-mark & 5-mark answers with balanced equations, ensuring zero marks lost.
