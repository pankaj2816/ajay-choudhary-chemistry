Core Specialty

Organic Chemistry

Reaction Mechanisms, Stereochemistry & Synthesis

View Study MaterialsView Question Papers

Organic Chemistry is taught with an unwavering focus on electron displacement effects, reaction intermediates (carbocations, carbanions, free radicals), and transition states. Instead of rote-learning 200+ reactions, students learn how to predict product formation using standard mechanistic rules.

Comprehensive Syllabus Modules Covered

General Organic Chemistry (GOC)

  • Inductive & Resonance Effects
  • Hyperconjugation & Aromaticity (Hückel’s Rule)
  • Carbocation, Carbanion & Radical Stabilities
  • Acidic & Basic Strength Comparisons

Reaction Mechanisms

  • Electrophilic Addition (Markovnikov & Anti-Markovnikov)
  • Nucleophilic Substitution (SN1 vs SN2)
  • Elimination Reactions (E1, E2, Saytzeff vs Hofmann)
  • Electrophilic Aromatic Substitution (Directing Groups)

Named Reactions & Conversions

  • Aldol & Cannizzaro Condensation
  • Reimer-Tiemann & Kolbe’s Synthesis
  • Wurtz, Fittig & Friedel-Crafts Reactions
  • Hell-Volhard-Zelinsky (HVZ) Reaction

Functional Groups & Biomolecules

  • Haloalkanes & Haloarenes
  • Alcohols, Phenols & Ethers
  • Carbonyl Compounds (Aldehydes, Ketones, Acids)
  • Amines, Diazonium Salts & Carbohydrates
Core Specialty

Inorganic Chemistry

Periodic Properties, Bonding & Coordination Chemistry

View Study MaterialsView Question Papers

Inorganic Chemistry is transformed from a memorization headache into a logical, structured discipline. We emphasize quantum mechanical foundations, shielding effects, lattice energies, and crystal field splitting to naturally explain trends and anomalous behaviors.

Comprehensive Syllabus Modules Covered

Periodic Classification & Trends

  • Effective Nuclear Charge (Zeff) & Slater’s Rules
  • Ionization Enthalpy & Electron Gain Enthalpy
  • Electronegativity Scales & Diagonal Relationships
  • Anomalous 2nd Period Elements Behavior

Chemical Bonding & MOT

  • VSEPR Theory & Lone Pair Repulsions
  • Hybridization (sp, sp², sp³, dsp², d²sp³)
  • Molecular Orbital Theory (MOT Energy Diagrams)
  • Dipole Moments & Hydrogen Bonding Types

Coordination Compounds

  • Werner’s Theory & IUPAC Nomenclature
  • Isomerism (Geometrical, Optical, Linkage, Ionization)
  • Crystal Field Theory (Octahedral & Tetrahedral Δ)
  • Magnetic Moment Calculations (Spin-Only BM)

Block Chemistry & Metallurgy

  • p-Block Hydrides, Oxoacids & Halides
  • d- & f-Block Transition Elements & Lanthanoid Contraction
  • Ellingham Diagrams & Metal Extraction Principles
