Class 12 · Chapter 4

The d- and f-Block Elements

Transition metal properties, why they are coloured and magnetic, KMnO₄ and K₂Cr₂O₇, and the lanthanoid contraction.

2 min read

The big idea

Transition metals have partly filled d orbitals. Those d electrons explain nearly every special property: many oxidation states, colours, magnetism, catalysis and complex formation.

Key properties and why

PropertyReason
Variable oxidation statesns and (n−1)d electrons have similar energies, so different numbers can be lost
Coloured ionsd–d transitions absorb visible light; the colour seen is the complementary one
Paramagnetismunpaired d electrons; μ = √(n(n+2)) BM
Catalytic activityvariable oxidation states and large surface area (Fe in the Haber process, V₂O₅ in the contact process)
High melting pointsstrong metallic bonding using d electrons
Interstitial compoundssmall atoms (H, C, N) fit in the gaps of the metal lattice

Zn²⁺, Cd²⁺ and Sc³⁺ are colourless: d¹⁰ or d⁰ means no d–d transition.

Two important compounds

Potassium dichromate (K₂Cr₂O₇)

  • Orange; strong oxidising agent in acid (Cr goes from +6 to +3).
  • Chromate (yellow) ⇌ dichromate (orange) depends on pH.

Potassium permanganate (KMnO₄)

  • Purple; strong oxidising agent.
  • Acidic medium: MnO₄⁻ → Mn²⁺ (5 electrons). Neutral or weakly basic: → MnO₂ (3 electrons).

f-block: lanthanoids and actinoids

  • Lanthanoid contraction: atomic and ionic sizes decrease steadily from La to Lu because 4f electrons shield poorly. Result: Zr and Hf have almost the same size and are hard to separate.
  • Common oxidation state of lanthanoids: +3 (also +2 and +4 in some, for stable f⁰, f⁷, f¹⁴).
  • Actinoids show more oxidation states and are radioactive.
Tip: Mn shows the most oxidation states in the 3d series (+2 to +7) because it has 5 unpaired d electrons plus 2 s electrons available.

Quick revision

  • Cu⁺ is unstable in water; it disproportionates to Cu²⁺ and Cu.
  • E° values: Mn³⁺/Mn²⁺ is high (Mn²⁺ d⁵ is stable); Cr³⁺ is stable in water (half-filled t₂g).
  • Misch metal (lanthanoid alloy) is used in lighter flints.
