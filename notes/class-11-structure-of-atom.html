Class 11 · Chapter 2

Structure of Atom

From Bohr's orbits to quantum numbers and orbitals, and how electrons fill them.

2 min read

The big idea

Electrons do not move in neat circular tracks. They exist in orbitals, regions where the chance of finding an electron is high. Four quantum numbers work like an address that tells you exactly which orbital and which spin an electron has.

Key ideas

  • Subatomic particles: proton (+1, in nucleus), neutron (0, in nucleus), electron (−1, outside).
  • Atomic number (Z) = number of protons. Mass number (A) = protons + neutrons.
  • Isotopes: same Z, different A (¹²C and ¹⁴C). Isobars: same A, different Z (⁴⁰Ar and ⁴⁰Ca).
  • Bohr model (H atom): energy of the nth orbit Eₙ = −13.6 / n² eV. Energy is released when an electron jumps down and absorbed when it jumps up.
  • Dual nature: electrons behave as particles and waves. de Broglie wavelength λ = h / mv.
  • Heisenberg uncertainty principle: you cannot know the exact position and exact momentum of an electron at the same time.

Quantum numbers: the electron's address

Quantum numberSymbolTells youValues
Principalnshell and size/energy1, 2, 3 …
Azimuthallsubshell shape (s, p, d, f)0 to n − 1
Magneticmₗorientation of the orbital−l to +l
Spinmₛspin direction+½ or −½

An s orbital is a sphere, p orbitals are dumbbells (pₓ, p_y, p_z), and d orbitals have more complex four-lobed shapes.

Filling electrons

  1. Aufbau principle: fill lower-energy orbitals first. Order by the (n + l) rule: 1s 2s 2p 3s 3p 4s 3d 4p 5s …
  2. Pauli exclusion principle: an orbital holds at most two electrons, with opposite spins.
  3. Hund's rule: in orbitals of equal energy, electrons first occupy them singly with parallel spins, then pair up.
Remember the exceptions: Cr is [Ar] 3d⁵ 4s¹ and Cu is [Ar] 3d¹⁰ 4s¹. Half-filled and fully-filled d subshells are extra stable.

Common mistakes

  • Writing 4s after 3d while filling. Fill 4s first, but remove 4s electrons first when forming ions (Fe²⁺ is [Ar] 3d⁶).
  • Mixing up "orbit" (Bohr's fixed path) and "orbital" (probability region).
  • Forgetting that the number of orbitals in a shell is n² and the maximum number of electrons is 2n².

Quick revision

  • Number of radial nodes = n − l − 1; angular nodes = l.
  • Spectral lines of hydrogen: Lyman (to n = 1, UV), Balmer (to n = 2, visible), Paschen (to n = 3, IR).
  • Photon energy E = hν = hc / λ.
