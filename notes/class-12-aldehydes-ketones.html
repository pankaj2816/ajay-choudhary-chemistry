Aldehydes, Ketones and Carboxylic Acids

The carbonyl group, nucleophilic addition, aldol and Cannizzaro, tests, and the acidity of carboxylic acids.

2 min read

The big idea

The C=O group is polar: carbon is δ+ and oxygen is δ−. So nucleophiles attack the carbon. That single idea, nucleophilic addition, explains most aldehyde and ketone reactions.

Reactivity

Aldehydes are more reactive than ketones:

  • fewer bulky groups around the carbon (less crowding),
  • fewer alkyl groups pushing electrons in (carbon stays more positive).

HCHO > CH₃CHO > CH₃COCH₃.

Key reactions of carbonyl compounds

  • Addition: HCN (cyanohydrins), NaHSO₃, Grignard reagents (alcohols), alcohols (acetals).
  • With NH₂–Z derivatives (addition–elimination): hydroxylamine → oxime, hydrazine → hydrazone, 2,4-DNP → orange/yellow precipitate (test for C=O).
  • Reduction: to alcohols (NaBH₄), to CH₂ by Clemmensen (Zn-Hg/HCl) or Wolff–Kishner (NH₂NH₂/KOH).
  • Aldol condensation: compounds with α-hydrogen + dilute NaOH → β-hydroxy aldehyde/ketone → loses water to give an α,β-unsaturated compound.
  • Cannizzaro reaction: aldehydes without α-hydrogen (HCHO, C₆H₅CHO) + conc. NaOH → one molecule oxidised to acid salt, one reduced to alcohol.

Tests to tell aldehydes from ketones

Tollens' reagentsilver mirrorno change
Fehling's solutionred precipitate (aliphatic aldehydes)no change
Iodoform (I₂ + NaOH)only CH₃CHOmethyl ketones (CH₃CO–) give yellow CHI₃

Carboxylic acids

  • Stronger acids than phenols and alcohols: the carboxylate ion has two equivalent resonance structures.
  • −I groups increase acidity: CCl₃COOH > CHCl₂COOH > CH₂ClCOOH > CH₃COOH. HCOOH is stronger than CH₃COOH.
  • HVZ reaction: Cl₂/red P puts Cl on the α-carbon. Decarboxylation: sodium salt + soda lime → alkane.
Tip: Before choosing aldol or Cannizzaro, check the α-carbon (the carbon next to C=O). α-H present → aldol. No α-H → Cannizzaro.

Quick revision

  • Rosenmund reduction: acid chloride → aldehyde (H₂, Pd-BaSO₄).
  • Etard reaction: toluene → benzaldehyde (CrO₂Cl₂).
  • Benzoic acid's −COOH is meta directing.
